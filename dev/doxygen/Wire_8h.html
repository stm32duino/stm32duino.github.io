◆ BUFFER_LENGTH

#define BUFFER_LENGTH   32

Minimal Rx/Tx buffer length in bytes.

Note
Buffers length is increased when needed, but the Tx buffer is limited to WIRE_MAX_TX_BUFF_LENGTH to avoid too much stack consumption. Buffer length and maximum buffer length are limited by the uint16_t type.

◆ WIRE_MAX_TX_BUFF_LENGTH

#define WIRE_MAX_TX_BUFF_LENGTH   1024U

Maximum Tx buffer length in bytes.

Can be overridden before including Wire.h.

Note
Limited by the uint16_t type.
