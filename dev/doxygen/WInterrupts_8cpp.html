Attach a callback function to an external interrupt on a pin.

Note
LOW and HIGH level triggers are only available on series supporting level interrupts, otherwise they fall back to FALLING and RISING respectively.
Parameters
pinThe pin to attach the interrupt to.
callbackFunction called when the interrupt fires. It takes no arguments.
modeTrigger mode: CHANGE, RISING, FALLING, LOW or HIGH.
