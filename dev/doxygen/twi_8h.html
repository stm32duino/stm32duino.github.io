Enumerator
I2C_OK 

Operation completed successfully.

I2C_DATA_TOO_LONG 

Data does not fit in the buffer.

I2C_NACK_ADDR 

Address was not acknowledged (NACK).

I2C_NACK_DATA 

Data was not acknowledged (NACK).

I2C_ERROR 

Other error.

I2C_TIMEOUT 

Operation timed out.

I2C_BUSY 

Bus or peripheral is busy.
